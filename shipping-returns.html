2. Returns & Refunds for Food Items

Because ghee is a consumable food product, maintaining hygiene and safety is our absolute priority. We cannot accept returns on opened food items, as they cannot be resold.

However, we offer replacements or refunds under the following specific conditions:

  • The jar was damaged, broken, or leaking upon arrival.
  • You received the incorrect product or size.
  • The product is defective or compromised.

3. How to Request a Replacement

If you face any of the issues above, please follow these steps strictly within 24 to 48 hours of delivery:

  1. Do not discard the packaging or the product.
  2. Take clear photographs and/or a video showing the damage, leakage, or incorrect item.
  3. Send the photos along with your Order ID to our WhatsApp at [WhatsApp] or email us at [Email].

Our team will review the claim and initiate a free replacement or a full refund to your original payment method (or bank account for COD orders) within 3-5 business days.
