Cow ghee is typically yellowish and has a milder flavor, making it great for everyday digestion and cooking. Buffalo ghee is white, thicker, and has a higher fat content, which is excellent for making traditional sweets or deep-frying.

Ghee does not need refrigeration. Store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Always use a clean, dry spoon to scoop the ghee, as moisture can spoil it.

Graininess (Danedar texture) is a sign of traditional, pure ghee. It happens because different fatty acids melt and solidify at different temperatures during the cooling process.
